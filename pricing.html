FAQ

Questions we always get.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No. The Starter plan is free forever and requires no payment details. Pro and Team plans include a 14-day free trial — no card needed until you decide to upgrade.

Can I switch plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your account settings. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

What happens when my free trial ends?

You'll be notified before your trial ends. If you don't add a payment method, your account automatically moves to the free Starter plan — you won't lose your data.

Do you offer discounts for African teams?

Yes — Africa-local pricing is available. BuildHive is committed to making collaboration accessible for teams across Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and beyond at pricing that makes sense locally.

What is the Team plan best for?

The Team plan is ideal for startups and growing companies with dedicated design and engineering teams. It includes everything you need to manage full product cycles together.

How does Enterprise pricing work?

Enterprise is custom-quoted based on team size, infrastructure needs, and required integrations. Contact us at hello@buildhive.site and we'll put together a proposal within 48 hours.
